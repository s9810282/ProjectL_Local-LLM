### 1.2 T3 admission and target-policy decisions

> Superseded on 2026-10-07: the user restricted empty-ground attacks and friendly fire to Cannon. Other categories may target hostile units, hostile structures, and explicitly attackable obstacles. See the [new policy design](../specs/2026-10-07-attack-target-policy-design.md) and [implementation plan](2026-10-07-attack-target-policy.md). The historical decisions below describe the previous behavior; this notice does not change runtime code.

**User-confirmed 2026-10-01:** Empty-cell aiming is allowed, including Single with no primary or surrounding targets. Allied units and the actor's own-side structures may be hit; the acting unit cannot take damage from its own attack. A selected Direction group with no eligible damageable target must not execute an attack. Cannon near-aim restrictions still apply before empty-cell permission.

- Single admission must not require an occupied primary cell. Preserve the selected PrimaryCell even when PrimaryTargetId is empty; collect eligible splash targets independently and assign Additional roles without inventing a primary entity. An empty CactusCannon center with an eligible neighbor is a valid attack.
- A legal Single aim with no damageable primary or surrounding targets is also allowed. Use the established successful-command cost path: charge normal attack AP once, subject to ordinary admission/AP checks; lack of targets is not itself a rejection. This follows the existing accepted-attack cost contract, not a separate user-confirmed free-attack exception. Zero target count does not automatically suppress an independently approved terrain effect.
- Allied units and allied structures are eligible both as directly selected targets and as indirect splash/direction targets. Direction admission must count eligible friendly targets, not just hostiles; a friendly-only group is not an empty group. Existing dead/undeployed/non-damageable checks remain applicable. ChoiceMode must not encode faction policy.
- An empty Direction group is a normal game-rule rejection: no attack AP charge, damage, success effect, gameplay mutation or ordinary turn advance. The command still receives the existing handled/rejection result metadata; Core does not clear View input. Eligible-target count is distinct from predicted positive HP damage, so this decision does not require damage to exceed zero.
- Explicitly exclude the acting EntityId from its own ordinary/splash/pierce attack damage and attack-triggered ThornField application damage, regardless of footprint overlap. This does not create immunity to existing fields crossed during voluntary/forced movement or unrelated external damage; those settlement rules remain unchanged. Neutral targets and empty All attacks remain unresolved. Do not generalize the Direction-only no-target rejection to Single.

Expected Task 7D acceptance: empty-center CactusCannon hits an eligible neighboring ally with the authored 70% Additional scale; an allied structure can be the primary; empty Single succeeds and charges normal attack AP once; a friendly-only Direction group proceeds and charges attack AP once; a Direction group with zero eligible targets rejects without gameplay changes or AP charge. Self overlap never damages the attacker. Verify result publication separately from gameplay changes. These decisions are recorded for future integration, not implemented by this document update.
