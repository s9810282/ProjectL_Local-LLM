# Tornado in the stage gimmick system

2026-11-01 · Core gameplay decisions recorded for implementation planning · Project_L

## 1. Gameplay contract

The user owns the Tornado Core change. Replace the current one-cell Tornado with the moving sandstorm described in section 5.1 of the supplied `projectl_gimmick_design_request.md` (slide 5 transcription). The existing static `StaticTerrainKind.Sandstorm` has different range and damage rules and is a separate feature. The source for this design is the supplied MD and the stage-gimmick implementation originally inspected at `2555a516b` with local changes. The turn-entry and preparation contracts were rechecked on `feature/Cheon/Core/NewCombatSytem` at `c3daac1fc` for the implementation plan. The original slides were not inspected.

| Subject | Rule | Basis |
|---|---|---|
| Clock | A player-side end adds one count, and an enemy-side end adds one. The storm moves every two counts by default. | Established common contract and slide 5 |
| Enemy turn end | Record the enemy turn end and increment the completed count once, then resolve the scheduled Tornado. Both remain in the committed result if a landing ends combat. | Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-01 |
| Storm | A persistent 3×3 square travels up to three diagonal steps measured from its center. Starting on the left sends it right, and starting on the right sends it left. Reverse horizontal travel at the side edge. The vertical direction is random. Shorten a move when the full distance is unavailable. | Slide 5 |
| Warning | Announce the direction before movement. The resolved motion follows that announcement. The player does not receive the future route. | Slides 3 and 5 |
| Contact during travel | Test the starting footprint, each intermediate footprint and the destination footprint. Throw a unit as soon as the moving storm touches it. | Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-01 |
| Repeat contact | Each storm may throw each unit at most once during its sweep, including landings caused by that sweep. A later entry after the sweep ends is a fresh contact. | Confirmed by the user on 2026-11-01 |
| Contact between scheduled moves | During combat, a unit entering the stationary footprint is thrown immediately. Its entry leaves the storm center, warning and movement deadline intact. | Immediate throw confirmed by the user. Separation from the periodic movement clock follows the two-count design. |
| Deployment and activation | Prepare the initial center once for the battle. During deployment it can be read as a stationary starting position. Tornado gameplay begins when combat entry succeeds. An existing overlap at activation is first evaluated on the next actual contact or scheduled sweep. | Design choice following the user’s deployment clarification on 2026-11-01 |
| Throw | Move the affected unit to a random legal tile on the map. This destination is selected independently of the storm's diagonal movement. The storm itself deals no authored damage. Existing landing effects can still change HP or end the battle. | Slide 5 and existing forced-arrival contract |
| Ownership | Scope covers Core rules, prepared input, state, Buffer publication, evidence and verification. The consumer contract in section 7 is sufficient for a later handoff. | User's Core-only scope |

**Example.** With zero-based coordinates, a storm moves its center from (2,2) through (3,3) and (4,4) to (5,5). A unit at (1,1) is touched at departure. A unit at (3,4) is touched at the first step even though neither endpoint footprint covers it. A unit at (6,6) is touched at arrival. A fourth unit walking into the footprint between activations is thrown on that movement step. Entering during count 1 leaves the scheduled move due at count 2.

## 3. Where the rules and state live

**Recommended approach.** Extend the implemented stage-gimmick system. A proposed `TornadoGimmickRule` implements the existing `IStageGimmickRule.TryAdvance` contract for scheduled movement. A feature-local contact routine handles storm-to-unit and unit-to-storm contact. A pure geometry routine computes footprints and legal diagonal steps. The coordinator owns ordering and composition. Tornado owns geometry, warning choices, victim selection and throws.

An alternative would register a thin adapter that still calls the old terrain calculation. That preserves one-cell source queries, but its `AfterActivation` contract couples a throw to source respawn and rescheduling. Extending that path would also couple the moving area to terrain placement/overlap validation. Moving the authoritative storm state to the existing gimmick collection makes entry and schedule calculations read the same area without installing nine independent terrain sources.

| Fact | Single owner and lifetime |
|---|---|
| Static tuning and placement | Preserve the existing stage placement and terrain-definition SO input path. Prepare one immutable `TornadoGimmickSpec` carrying the configured storm instances. Defaults are a 3×3 square, 3 diagonal steps, first/repeat at 2 counts, and warning lead 1. Use a positive odd side length, positive distance/intervals and a lead in 1..min(first, repeat). Keep the serialized placement ID and observer visibility. |
| Per-storm runtime facts | One `TornadoGimmickState` in `CombatCommandState.Gimmicks`. A single configured storm needs center, horizontal travel sign, warning or travel phase, next boundary count, and a locked diagonal direction while warned. If the stage has several authored source IDs, store an ordered collection of these members under the one Tornado kind. The collection's next boundary is the minimum member deadline, derived when constructing the immutable value. |
| Geometry | Derive cells from center and the prepared footprint size in one pure routine. It serves contact, preparation validation and the public area projection. Keep unit membership out of character status storage. |
| Turn and randomness | Existing completed-controller count and `EnvironmentProgressFacts.RandomState`. Sample through one local environment RNG cursor and publish its final state with the resulting transition. |
| Unit state | Existing character position, occupancy, immunity, HP and arrival effects. Each throw updates the current projection before choosing the next unit's destination. |
| Resolution-local data | Actual path prefix, current step center, pending contacts and a per-source/per-entity contact guard. Keep the guard through the sweep and its nested landings. Discard it when the sweep ends. An independent stationary entry gets a fresh guard for its landing chain. Publish only the historical evidence needed to describe what happened. |

Remove migrated Tornado placements from `Environment.Tiles` and terrain bindings at the conversion boundary. The old persisted source and the new gimmick state cannot both be live for the same authored ID. Keep the long authored source ID for throw attribution, distinct from the battle-local `StageGimmickId` assigned by definition index. Appending the definition preserves existing indices even though the rule executes first. Retain source-ID uniqueness and reserved-ID checks for inputs using the legacy namespace.

The current rule interface does not supply the `IMovementTileRule` list required for real landing effects. **Proposed wiring:** pass the already validated movement-rule list from the turn calculator into the production composition entry and bind it to the Tornado rule at composition time. Keep the per-rule progression signature. Use a pure registration list with the existing three handlers, and carry the bound composition through the same combat invocation. Preserve existing Railway-only compatibility entry points. A second default list would silently change landing behavior for custom/injected tile rules. Verify this seam with a distinctive injected arrival rule through the actual turn-end path.

## 4. Movement, warning and contact algorithm

The user accepted the edge, placement and multi-storm policies below. The deployment qualification uses the existing combat phase and applies to both initial placement and subsequent deployment.

1. **Prepare.** A random placement picks a left or right edge start where the entire square fits. Pick a center on that edge with the battle's environment RNG and set the horizontal sign inward. For explicit placement, treat the saved cell as the center, require the entire square to fit, and derive the inward sign from the chosen side. For an interior or exact middle start, require an authored starting side to resolve ambiguity. Reject an impossible footprint before installing battle state, with the stage and source ID in the error. Keep board dimensions data-driven.
2. **Warn.** At the boundary one count before travel, take the existing horizontal sign, reversing it if the footprint is already on its outgoing horizontal edge. Choose up/down once from signs permitting diagonal progress. Where only one sign works, use it. For each candidate, test full-footprint containment at successive centers until the SO distance is reached or another step would leave the board. Use unit walkability only for *landings*. Storm motion can cross internal walls, cliffs, units or structures. Publish only the chosen direction for observers entitled to see the warning. If no diagonal step exists, record a stationary activation and expose no misleading arrow. If warning lead equals the first interval, prepare the first direction with the initial state and expose the active warning at combat activation.
3. **Travel.** On the due boundary, use the locked direction and walk those diagonal steps. For a nominal distance of three, the history has the origin and up to three reached centers. A shortened move ends at the last valid center. Reaching the horizontal edge changes the horizontal sign for the *next* move. At a vertical edge choose a feasible sign at the next warning. A zero-step activation checks the current footprint once. When warning lead equals repeat interval, establish the following warning in the same committed boundary after the current activation.
4. **Sweep contacts.** At each center, including the origin, obtain living field characters intersecting the current footprint in the current projected world. Order simultaneous candidates by `EntityId`. Recheck each prior to moving it, as an earlier landing can kill, move or remove it. One storm throws a given unit at most once during this sweep, even if it touches several consecutive footprints or lands in the route ahead. Immediately before an actual throw, record the pair (storm source ID, unit ID) in the sweep's guard so its landing can safely run contact checks. A later independent entry or activation starts a fresh resolution. A placed unit remaining in a stationary area is not re-evaluated on idle ticks.
5. **Stationary entry.** Authorize gameplay contact only in an ongoing `StagePhase.Combat`. An accepted combat movement step or forced entry into the footprint can throw its entrant immediately. Deployment placement, repositioning and pre-entry enemy redeployment are inert. `EnterCombat` performs that redeployment before committing `CombatEntered`. Use the accepted phase transition to activate the existing prepared storm. Activation itself is not an arrival or a sweep. A unit already inside can stay there until the first sweep, or trigger a new contact by entering a footprint during combat. An actual throw interrupts the original route. Entry preserves the storm center, direction and deadline.
6. **Land.** Skip a unit protected by existing forced-movement immunity. For supported units, enumerate in-board legal destinations in stable row-major order. Exclude the departure cell, living unit/structure occupancy, walls and cliffs using the shared walkable-terrain rule. Apply any actual unit-specific standing restriction, independently of action availability, movement range or path reachability. Choose once from that set. If empty, the unit stays. A unit displaced earlier frees its old tile and occupies its new one for subsequent draws. Keep the current multi-cell support boundary explicit: immune multi-cell units are skipped, and a movable multi-cell unit requires verified whole-footprint occupancy/placement support before enabling that content.
7. **Settle.** Reuse `MovementProjection.RecordForcedMove`, forced-terrain entry, presence reconciliation, landing object/soul effects and the existing death/outcome settlement. An immediate re-entry into the same storm by the same throw is suppressed. Share the guard across all nested landings caused by this sweep or standalone entry. A second storm may throw that unit once for its own contact. The scheduled sweep evaluates every source due at the boundary in stable source-ID order. If a landing ends combat, stop at the actual path prefix, omit unperformed contacts and later gimmicks, and publish the terminal result once. Only successful non-terminal scheduling sets the next normal deadline.

For overlapping storms, the agreed tie-break is source ID on entry and for simultaneous scheduled moves. Contact membership uses each storm's **projected current center**, including the currently traversed center. At a nested landing, consulting an old origin or an already planned destination would produce different victims. Pass the in-progress geometry and guard within the movement projection. The committed Buffer remains immutable during calculation.

**Forced-entry call sites.** Tile-forced displacement in `MovementEnvironmentCalculator.ResolveForcedMovement`, [attack displacement](../../../Assets/ProjectL/Script/Systems/RuntimeSystem/InGameSystem/CoreSystem/CoreCaculator/Application/Combat/AttackForcedMovement.cs) and [attack knockback](../../../Assets/ProjectL/Script/Systems/RuntimeSystem/InGameSystem/CoreSystem/CoreCaculator/Application/Combat/AttackKnockbackCalculator.cs) each have their own per-step pipeline. Wire the same contact routine into an actual reached step after its fall/thorn checks, before the next displacement step or deferred object processing. Recheck the unit's anchor, life and terminal state after the call. If Tornado displaced it, end the original shove/pull and let the throw own its landing. Running the entire ordinary-entry pipeline here would repeat effects already handled by these callers. Verify an attack knockback and a tile-forced move entering a stationary storm mid-path. Verify an interrupted pull cannot complete a follow-up from the obsolete endpoint. Treat undo/restoration as restoration. A forward Position Swap already calls `ResolveEntry` for both arrivals in entity-ID order. Use that one call for each participant, recheck the actual landing anchor before any subsequent arrival settlement, and cover paired undo separately.

For a supported one-cell victim, [MovementRouteValidator.CanOccupy](../../../Assets/ProjectL/Script/Systems/RuntimeSystem/InGameSystem/CoreSystem/CoreCaculator/Domain/Movement/MovementRouteValidator.cs) already combines board bounds, blocked voluntary terrain and current unit/structure occupancy. Supply the thrown unit as the excluded occupant and remove its departure cell separately. Its projected location, rather than the command issuer's location or the storm center, supplies the origin of the throw.

## 5. Confirmed enemy turn-end order

Today the enemy-end path runs legacy Tornado *before* emitting `EnemyPhaseEnded`. If a landing ends combat there, the enemy-end event and count increment are absent. The player-end path already retains its increment in the corresponding case.

The user confirmed the following order on 2026-11-01. Record `EnemyPhaseEnded` and increment `CompletedControllerTurnCount` once in the projected transition. Evaluate scheduled Tornado using that updated count. Publish the combined result through the existing transaction. If a landing ends combat, retain the enemy-end event and its increment, record the actual Tornado and landing events through the terminal outcome, and stop further progression. An ordinary move into a stationary storm advances the clock only when the side subsequently completes its turn.

## 6. Migration and compatibility

- **Authoring.** Keep stage-authored fixed/random starts, source identity and visibility. Add storm footprint size and diagonal distance to the existing Tornado authoring definition, and map existing first/repeat fields into the immutable spec. Change the named default asset's serialized 3/3 to 2/2 intentionally after inventory. Review custom values individually and diagnose invalid old centers on small maps. Do not silently move hand-authored centers. Inventory actual stage references, development fixtures and multiple-source inputs before content conversion.
- **Runtime.** Add the Tornado key and typed grouped state to preparation, state equality and validation. Preserve shared RNG when composing the movement projection with the updated storm state. Convert or retire `InitialTornadoPlacementCalculator`'s single-cell materialization, `CommandEnvironmentState.ChangeTornado`'s old successor check and random source respawn. Both legacy periodic calls disappear when the new rule is registered. The existing entry site then tests the new footprint. A stage with no Tornado keeps its prior RNG sequence.
- **Preview, visibility and undo.** Preview simulates the same contact and legal destinations on a disposable projection using the observer's permitted facts. Capture the actual relevant storm geometry/ID, visible eligibility, landing candidates/blockers and RNG in movement-context freshness checks. The current code tests for `CombatTornadoEvent` or a `Relocated` event with `TornadoSourceId`; update that evidence to the new event family. A parked storm touching a path can consume RNG even when its own center does not move. Verify existing forced-relocation/undo barriers through the actual publication path. A preview or rejected/stale command cannot consume RNG or move a storm.
- **Validation.** Extend `StageGimmickStateSet.ValidateDefinitions` and `CombatChangeEvidenceReader.ValidateGimmickChanges` for the new kind. Preserve fail-closed handling of unknown variants. Replay typed expected/final facts in event order and match the installed state. Verify boundary, source identity, actual path prefix, legal diagonal deltas, contact origin and per-activation uniqueness. Validate each relocation against its matching before/after character facts. The feature calculator alone selects routes, victims and RNG draws. Have the Buffer enforce integrity without repeating those selections.
- **Prior consumers.** Existing `CombatTornadoEvent.Expected/Final` are one-cell terrain facts. Existing `Relocated` carries a long source ID. Introduce typed storm warning/travel evidence, retain the source ID on the actual relocation, and distinguish stationary entry from a sweep step. Expose the step index or equivalent ordered contact evidence so several throws on one sweep are unambiguous. Specify recycling/deprecation of old payloads according to actual retained consumers. Old and new events must never cause two throws for one contact. Runtime activation on production stages requires compatible consumers.

## 7. Core read and result contracts

Proposed public meanings, with exact type names left to implementation:

| Consumer need | Core supplies |
|---|---|
| Bind or reattach during a battle | Existing Combat Buffer read sink exposes the Tornado member's stable ID, visible current area, phase, and locked warning direction when announced. Derive the area from authoritative center and size inside one read projection. |
| Read the starting position during deployment | Expose the same prepared center/area subject to observer visibility. Its gameplay activity is derived from the authoritative combat phase. Reading it consumes no RNG. Whether a consumer shows the area is its owner’s choice. |
| A warning becomes active | A committed direction-only warning for that source and boundary. Earlier read requests cannot choose the direction. |
| A storm moves | Source ID and the **actual** ordered center sequence, including departure, plus ordered contact/relocation facts. Keep a historical path for the move that already occurred. A terminal result includes only reached centers. |
| A unit enters a stationary storm | One attributed forced relocation and the normal ordered landing results. No storm-travel event or schedule reset occurs. |
| Finish or cancel presentation | Existing result sequencing and acknowledgement apply. Re-reading a result cannot alter effects, RNG or turn progression. |

These contracts are the Core portion of eventual integration. Production display, audio, camera, prefab and scene work remain with their owners.

Publish one expected/final grouped-state change for the configured Tornado kind per boundary. Its ordered member records distinguish warnings, reached centers and their source IDs. Link each interleaved relocation to the source and step that caused it. This allows the evidence reader to reconstruct the group exactly once even if two authored storms are due together. Standalone entry produces relocation and arrival evidence while the grouped state compares equal before and after. A throw with no eligible landing consumes no destination draw and emits no relocation to a fictional cell.

## 8. Acceptance and change locality

| Scenario | Observable result and regression boundary |
|---|---|
| Known seed, 2/2 schedule, repeated reads | Initial area -> direction warning at 1 -> matching travel at 2 -> warning at 3 -> travel at 4. Both side ends count. Unit actions, queries and preview leave the clock intact. Include non-default SO values. |
| Fatal landing on enemy turn end | Start at count 1 with Tornado due at 2. Assert exactly one `EnemyPhaseEnded` with final count 2 before the Tornado effects. A lethal landing produces one terminal outcome, the installed count remains 2, and progression stops before the next gimmick or turn. |
| Edges and constrained maps | Verify all four diagonals, shortened travel, horizontal reversal on the following move, a single valid vertical sign, zero usable diagonal steps, and failure to fit the footprint. Every reached footprint fits the real board. |
| Four contact examples from section 1 | Origin-only, intermediate-only and destination-only units are thrown at their first touched center. A stationary entrant is thrown on entry. One victim in multiple footprints or landing back in the same storm produces exactly one relocation attributed to that storm during the sweep. After the sweep ends, a new voluntary entry by that unit produces one further relocation immediately, with the storm center and deadline intact. |
| Deployment to combat | Repeated queries, accepted deployment placements and pre-entry redeployment leave Tornado facts and its RNG contribution intact. Rejected combat entry stays inert. Successful combat entry permits future contact without rescanning occupants. Default first warning/first sweep occur at counts 1/2. Re-entering combat with the same battle preserves the existing center and deadline. |
| Landing and arrival | RNG replay chooses the same legal cell, immune units stay, no-candidate units stay, dynamic occupancy is respected, and a cliff never becomes an initial landing. Landing effects, secondary forced entry, deaths and no attack credit follow existing contracts. |
| Several sources and stacked gimmicks | Multiple legacy IDs remain distinguishable under one common registration. Nested A -> B -> A terminates. A terminal effect stops later centers/sources and other gimmicks. Verify Tornado -> Railway -> Haboob -> Rockfall and Railway's existing death-event grouping. |
| Movement previews and Buffer retry | Same inputs predict the same movement, a changed relevant area/RNG invalidates stale preview, and rejected or retried transactions cannot publish partial movement or duplicate effects. Verify both entry and boundary paths using real Buffer transactions. |
| Migration and ownership | Test explicit and random authored starts, visibility, multiple IDs, invalid legacy cells and preserved custom profiles. Run affected Core, CoreIntegration and DB asset checks. Record consumer readiness separately from Core acceptance. |

Changing storm speed or interval touches its SO and data validation. Changing bounce geometry touches the Tornado geometry rule and edge tests. Changing who is thrown touches contact, landing and preview/evidence tests. Replacing an event contract additionally requires its consuming integration tests. Existing Railway, Haboob and Rockfall internals are only relevant for shared ordering, terminal short-circuit and transaction regressions.

**Decision record.** The user approved full-footprint containment, feasible vertical directions with horizontal reversal on the next move, travel across internal obstacles, seeded edge or authored starts, and independent throws by multiple storms in source-ID order. Deployment behavior is the planning choice described in section 1. Battle preparation may sample an initial position once. Use the current combat phase for contact eligibility and existing completed-side boundaries for progression. This policy requires no Tornado-owned day/night state, reset hook or transition timer. The [Core implementation plan](../plans/2026-11-01-tornado-stage-gimmick-implementation-plan.md) defines the implementation and review boundaries.
