## 1. Authority and method

Highest authority is the user's confirmed decisions: [pillar/platform decisions](2026-11-16-pillar-platform-decisions.md), [piercing/Dead Hand design §2](2026-11-14-piercing-and-dead-hand-design.md), and [main selection design](2026-11-15-run-start-main-selection-core-design.md). Older feature designs are context, not evidence of current behavior.

Review method: three independent read-only source reviews (obstacle durability and pillars; moving platforms; piercing and Dead Hand). The author then re-traced every High and Medium finding in the code; findings that did not hold were dropped. Low findings were spot-checked (several traced, marked "Verified" in their rows); the remaining Low rows are reviewer-reported and must be re-read when the fix is implemented. No Unity run or test execution was performed for the review. "Verified" in this document means source inspection, not runtime evidence.

Review criteria follow the repository rules: correctness against confirmed decisions, preview/execution parity, Buffer evidence validation, SSOT, SOLID, and change independence (a fix to one feature should not require reading or re-verifying unrelated features).
