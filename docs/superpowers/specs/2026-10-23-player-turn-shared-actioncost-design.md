## 1. Requirements and decisions

Labels throughout this document:

- **[Confirmed]**: the user's requirements, including the subsequent clarifications in this design session.
- **[Existing]**: behavior established by the inspected source; not an approval of future behavior.
- **[Proposed]**: the recommended design, subject to design approval.
- **[Open]**: a gameplay/content choice still requiring an answer. Missing answers do not prevent documenting the rest of the migration.

### 1.1 Confirmed changes

1. **[Confirmed]** One shared player ActionCost pool replaces every player's per-unit pool. Its initial maximum is **10**. Reset current AP to 10 when the player's own turn begins. Selecting another unit never restores it.
2. **[Confirmed]** Each unit may perform **one movement action OR one attack action per turn**. Repeating movement, repeating attack, and mixing the two are all disallowed after that unit's committed action. This restriction belongs to each unit separately: A acting does not consume B's action opportunity. Only ActionCost is shared. This resolves the repetition question in the original prompt.
3. **[Confirmed]** Remove unit initiative and its activation order as gameplay authority. Completing a unit action does not complete the player turn. The completed-turn counter observes **player-turn completion**, not individual units and not a player-plus-enemy round.
4. **[Confirmed]** Delete checkpoint return. It is not a temporarily disabled feature: remove its command, preview, refund behavior, UI/input integration, and obsolete reservation state.
5. **[Confirmed]** Retain health, attack, attack area, attack range, and priority. Priority's new mechanical role is not settled by retaining the stat.
6. **[Confirmed]** Movement allowance and spending are distinct. Orthogonal edges count 1; diagonal edges count 2. A permitted player movement action costs exactly 2 ActionCost, independent of the number of traversed cells. A diagonal is an L-shaped route: at least one of its two orthogonal decompositions must be legal. The chosen middle cell is actually traversed, including its mine/item/terrain effects; commit follows the path shown by preview.
7. **[Confirmed]** The player acts first, followed by the enemy phase, alternating while combat continues. Enemies have **no AP budget**, shared or per-unit. Each eligible enemy attacks if it has a legal target from its current position, otherwise attempts movement, at most once in its phase. It cannot move and then attack in that phase. Enemy movement still obeys category allowance and geometry.
8. **[Confirmed]** The player may end their turn early. Automatic player-turn ending is a future consideration and is not included, even at zero AP or with no remaining legal action.
9. **[Confirmed]** Each unit's action opportunity resets at the start of its own side's turn. A unit created during a turn waits until the next start of its own side's turn; changing selection never resets eligibility.
10. **[Confirmed]** Each night has **8 player turns**. After the eighth player turn finishes, execute the corresponding enemy phase, then change to day. Do not change to day after the eighth individual unit action or before the enemy's response. Connect existing night cleanup to this boundary; leave content with unresolved timing/meaning unavailable rather than designing new lifetimes for it.
11. **[Confirmed]** This is a systems migration. Traits, items and gimmicks are expected to undergo extensive deletion or change under a separate planner-approved content design. Migrate the underlying turn/action/state contracts and safely disconnect incompatible capabilities; do not redesign their effects, balance, triggers or durations, or promise that every legacy feature will return. Pending content decisions do not block system delivery. Night/day cycling remains in scope. This clarification is not a concrete content-removal list and does not authorize deleting content assets.

- **[Open]** Boss movement allowance remains unspecified. Preserve the current boss's movement-disabled behavior; decide an allowance only before enabling a movable Boss.
- **[Proposed]** Preserve action-scoped multi-hit/follow-up/reaction effects and forced displacement as resolution of an existing cause, not additional voluntary commands. The planner may delete, replace or redesign them. Existing definitions and unaffected calculations can remain untouched while capabilities are unavailable; preserving their future functionality is not a system-delivery requirement.

Shared maximum, enemy funding/order, manual end, eligibility reset, eight-turn day transition and diagonal legality are no longer open questions. Checkpoint return remains a confirmed deletion.

### 2.2 Actual execution and publication path

**[Existing] Entry:** LifeCycle entry requests reach `L/Plans/CombatPlan.RegisterEntry` and `L/Jobs/CombatPrepareJob`. `CS/BattleEntryService.EnterBattle` validates stage entry, captures Run/deck/roster sources, reads `DatabaseBattleInitializationSource`, calls `C/Application/Combat/BattlePreparationCalculator.Prepare`, revalidates sources, and installs through `CS/BattleLifetime` and `B/Core/BattleTransactions.PrepareBattle`. `Current` stage selection, character membership, and prepared battle install together. Loading/scene attachment follows through the existing entry transition; gameplay does not begin in Bootstrap by writing Buffer fields.

**[Existing] Progress:** `L/CombatLifeCycle` requests `JobType.Next`; `L/Plans/CombatPlan.Decide` reads `CombatProgressFacts`, prioritizes completion/abandonment, then commands, previews, and automatic advancement. Unpresented results prevent the next mutating step. `CombatCommandJob` checks handled command numbers; `CombatAdvanceJob` requires a newer accepted `Advance` result. Job completion is not equivalent to command acceptance. `CS/CombatCommandService`, `CombatPreviewService`, and `CombatAdvanceService` have separate admission/translation responsibilities and share `CombatInputAssembler`/`CombatTransitionPublisher`.

```text
View input -> CombatViewBuffer numbered request
                  |
CombatLifeCycle -> CombatPlan -> existing JobRunner / combat Job
                  -> role-specific Core service -> immutable input
                  -> pure rule calculation -> CombatCommandTransition
                  -> BattleTransactions: prepare every store, then install
                  -> result/history + Buffer facts -> View playback + acknowledgement
```

**[Existing] State ownership:** `B/Character/CharacterData.cs` exports immutable character facts from Character Buffer. `B/Combat/CombatStateBuffer.cs` owns current battle state, battle instance ID, handled/preview/result numbers, completion, and result history. `B/Core/BattleTransactions.Apply(CombatCommandTransition)` rejects already-handled command numbers, validates expected character/world/item/reward sources, prepares all changes, then installs characters, soul field, rewards, completion, inventory, and finally command state/result. `CombatStateBuffer.PrepareCommand` compares `BattleInstanceId` and full expected `CommandState`, validates placement/creation, and reserves the result sequence before installation. This is the reuse point for atomic budget and eligibility changes.

**[Existing] Selection/turn:** `CombatTurnCalculator.EnterCombat` registers all living field units; `BeginNext` chooses a participant using `CombatTurnOrder.Ordered`, installs `ActiveActorId`, refills that unit's AP, resets movement, and dispatches turn-start effects. Movement and attack admission require the requested actor to equal that active ID. `EndTurn` refills the ending unit again, schedules its next appearance, increments global completed unit turns, and adds its `Delay` to night time. Enemy AI can move, then attack, then call the same end-turn calculation inside one transition.

**[Existing] Outcome/exit:** `CombatCommandCalculator.Finish`, `CommandDeathSettlement`, and `BattleOutcomeCalculator` settle death, soul/drop effects, kill rewards, victory experience, and completion facts. `CombatTransitionPublisher` attaches progression and the receipt; Buffer installs them atomically. `CombatPlan` waits for presentation/confirmation and runs the existing completion/cleanup/Run-transition Jobs. Continuing runs go to StageSelect; ended runs prepare a new Run and go to Lobby. Boss completion has an earlier chapter-completion path before showing final results. This migration must not replace those paths.

### 2.4 Specific findings that affect design

**Movement is already applied, but provisionally reversible.** `RecordEnteredStep` changes AP, remaining range, pending distance/cost and refund-eligible cost; the published route moves the character and applies entry/arrival effects. Player movement is sealed on return barriers, attacks, turn end, or displacement; enemies seal immediately. `CheckpointReturnCalculator` refunds pending costs and restores the checkpoint, while movement traits/effects are not generally rolled back. Therefore “just make attack optional” leaves a second movement lifecycle in place. The migration must make each move final and delete the old return ledger.

**Live reach uses path length, not just displacement.** `MovementRouteValidator.Validate` checks every edge and accumulated steps; `Range` explores four orthogonal directions with `(distance,cost)` labels. `MovementDestination` also reports Manhattan displacement from the checkpoint, but that is not the live route allowance. Detours spend their actual path length. The separate `MovementPathCalculator.ResolvePathCells` walks toward a destination and is called by `AttackKnockbackCalculator` and `MovementEnvironmentCalculator` for displacement/contact resolution; it is not the voluntary-route authority. Preserve those callers rather than replacing explicit voluntary routes with that helper. New diagonals require adapting the real route validator, range search, AI search and View draft construction.

**Terrain currently changes AP.** `EnvironmentTerrainRules.EdgeActionCost` adds 2 for entering high ground from non-high ground. That directly conflicts with fixed 2 per movement action; remove that AP surcharge from active movement. Retain high-ground attack/defense/knockback rules. Do not silently convert the surcharge into movement units.

**Timeline UI retirement is partial.** The old `TimelineUI` has already been removed (see `T/Framework/CombatTimelineRetirementBootSmokeTests`). However, `CombatViewSystem` still calls `ICombatTimelineQuery.GetTimeline`; `FieldUnitPresenter.SetHudTimeline` and `UnitHUD.SetTurnOrder` still show order badges. `UnitVisualDataSO.timelineIcon` also remains. Removal must follow actual consumers, not the old screen's name.

**TimelineSeed is not exclusively a timeline seed.** `BattleEntryService` uses it for initial draw, `BattlePreparationCalculator` for initial tornado/environment setup, `BattlePreparation` for item RNG, and `CombatExperienceSettlement`/`MainGrowthRules.Choose` for deterministic main growth. Preserve these deterministic inputs. A rename to battle seed can migrate existing serialized values; deleting or rerolling them would alter unrelated behavior.

## 3. Proposed ownership and contracts

### 3.1 Smallest coherent structure

**[Proposed]** Extend the current immutable facts and existing Buffer transaction. Keep `CombatCommandService`, `CombatAdvanceService`, `CombatPreviewService`, the publisher, and LifeCycle Jobs. Replace the unit-turn calculations in `CombatTurnCalculator` with controller-turn lifecycle calculations. Keep category action policy beside category rules, path policy beside movement, and each deferred effect with its existing owner.

Two material alternatives were considered:

- A new budget System/Service and independent action store would create cross-store coordination and registrations for state that has exactly the battle lifetime. Reuse `CombatStateBuffer` instead.
- A new Core scheduler/Plan/Job framework would duplicate the existing LifeCycle Plan/Job path and risk retaining hidden unit turns. Use immutable next-phase facts and existing automatic steps instead. Sequential enemy action resolution is necessary execution ordering, not a schedule of unit turn appointments.

There is no need for a generic action bus, transaction framework, feature-flag service, or strategy registry. One small pure category-action rule and a shared admission calculation are justified by actual movement/attack/preview/AI consumers. They need no service interface merely to forward calls.

|---|---|---|
| Fixed rule configuration | Proposed pure `C/Contracts/Common/PlayerTurnRules` supplies confirmed maximum 10 and 8 player turns per night; immutable battle facts carry them through current initialization | One policy source, no new SO/table/service or per-stage duplicate. Validate before battle install; no progression adjustment in initial scope. These replace old unit-max/delay-based-night rules rather than adding a second authority. |
| Shared player current/maximum | One budget value in `CombatCommandState`, stored by `CombatStateBuffer`; maximum is the installed immutable rule value | Prepare inactive/zero current during deployment; each player turn starts at 10 exactly once. Enemy actions, selection, stat queries and display never refill it. Day does not itself refill: the next player turn does. Clear on EndBattle/new battle. **Configuration versus derived data.** **[Proposed]** Add one pure `CategoryActionRules` policy under `C/Domain/Categories` for the confirmed category table, usable by existing preparation/stat-query/calculation routes. It returns movement allowance and ordinary action costs; it neither reads Buffer nor owns current resources. Retire authorable per-unit copies for these fixed values in `UnitData`/`UnitSpec` and enhancement overrides. Published unit stats may expose derived allowance/action prices for cards, but cannot become independently editable policy sources. If category tuning becomes data-driven later, the existing `CategoryConfigData`/`CategorySpec` route is suitable; do not keep both hard-coded and editable authorities. Boss remains explicitly unresolved, never inferred from an old `moveRange: 0` asset.

Access the category policy through the existing Application preparation/stat-query calculations and immutable Contracts results. DB exporters supply category identity, not a new direct Systems-to-Domain dependency. Preserve the current assembly boundaries; no new asmdef reference is justified by this policy.

**Eligibility lifetime:** **[Confirmed]** initial/deferred units become available only at their controller start; a new summon waits for its next owner start. **[Proposed]** Death does not refund the shared pool or end the controller turn; later redeployment uses the same entity identity and receives eligibility only at the appropriate subsequent owner start. Selection changes and forced movement preserve action use. No active combat revive, ownership-transfer or replacement API was found in the inspected character/combat paths; do not build one for this migration. If introduced later, preserve same-entity consumption, defer newly created/transferred units until the next owner start, and admit it through the same transaction. Main persistent state and run roster changes must not regenerate combat actions. Existing noncombat roster acquisition/recovery remain separate lifetimes.

### 3.2 Ordinary action contract

The following names describe required semantics, not a detailed implementation plan.

| Boundary | Inputs / preconditions | Output / mutation / failure semantics |
|---|---|---|
| View command | Numbered `Move(actor,path,context)`, `Attack(actor,aim)`, or actor-free `EndPlayerTurn`; add expected battle ID and controller-turn sequence to mutating combat intent | Queue only. ### 3.3 Movement is a complete action

**[Confirmed]** Route reach counts orthogonal movement as 1 and diagonal movement as 2; the maximum is the actor's category allowance. A diagonal from `(x,y)` to `(x+dx,y+dy)` has two candidate routes: through `(x+dx,y)`, or through `(x,y+dy)`. It is legal if **at least one complete L route** is legal. One blocked middle cell does not block the other route. If neither works, reject. Attack ray corner-cut geometry is unrelated.

**[Proposed implementation]** Normalize a diagonal gesture into two explicit orthogonal steps before full-route validation. Reuse the current orthogonal path/search facilities rather than introducing a second diagonal execution model. If a draft already names its middle cell, preserve it. Otherwise choose a legal candidate deterministically (X-then-Y before Y-then-X when both are legal), publish the expanded route in preview, and bind commit to that exact route. Do not silently select the other middle cell at commit when the previewed route becomes stale. Candidate selection must consider complete-route constraints such as repeated cells, not just each isolated corner.

**[Confirmed]** The selected middle cell is a real entered cell. Apply its ordinary entry effects, objects and terrain behavior in route order; apply arrival-only effects only where the existing arrival contract says the unit actually stops. Do not apply effects from the unchosen middle cell. A mine, forced displacement or death at the chosen middle cell may stop movement there before the diagonal destination. Preview shows the exact public path and known effects; hidden-information guarantees remain. Both human input and AI consume the same normalized-path contract.

Preserve whole-route rejection for mismatched origin, repeated cells, blocking occupants, walls/cliffs and range overflow. Reject origin-only movement as `SameAnchor` without spending or consuming the action. `MovementDestination`/`MovePreview` expose actual orthogonal-route length (therefore 2 for a normalized diagonal) and the fixed player price separately. Do not substitute Manhattan displacement for route length on detours. Use normalized path indices for existing entered-cell/direction logic. Keep current single-cell admission; extending multi-cell characters, hidden-Cliff gameplay or attack wall geometry is outside scope.

For an accepted route interrupted after movement begins, **[Proposed]** retain the reached state and charge the player one fixed 2, including an interruption on a diagonal's middle cell; no partial distance refund. An enemy move consumes only its action opportunity. If the entire route is rejected before commitment, charge zero and preserve eligibility. Entry/arrival effects, death/reward settlement and final stop remain ordered through `MovementProjection`; adapt its resource bookkeeping, not its effect ordering wholesale.

**Confirmed deletion inventory:** remove `ReturnToCheckpoint` from command/preview producers and dispatch; `CheckpointReturnCalculator`, `CheckpointReturnPreview`, `CheckpointReturnResult`; `MovementCheckpointAnchor`, `PendingMoveActionCost`, `PendingMoveDistance`, `AttackRefundEligibleMoveCost` and their publication/validation/display paths. `CommittedMoveDistance`/`RemainingMoveRange` no longer authorize a second action: retain only a read-model value if a real display needs it, derived from category allowance and eligibility. Remove attack/end-turn/forced-displacement “seal pending movement” calls. Preserve explicit origin/last-valid/death-position data inside the existing action projection where effects need it; audit `AttackKnockbackCalculator`, `BossRoarCalculator`, `CommandDeathSettlement`, `FlagDropCalculator`, `SoulEffectCalculator` before removing checkpoint fallbacks. Do not replace those position facts with fabricated checkpoint state.

Deployment `ReturnUnit`/hand placement and night recall are different features from checkpoint return. Both remain; recall is attached to the day transition after enemy phase 8. Canceling an unsubmitted path draft also remains.

## 4. Turn lifecycle and deferred features

### 4.1 Controller lifecycle, not unit initiative

**[Confirmed sequence; Proposed placement into existing transitions]**:

1. Prepare battle and perform ordinary daytime deployment. `EnterCombat` validates objective/placement, captures reward participants through the existing path, redeploys waiting enemies, and records `NightStartPlayerTurnCount = CompletedPlayerTurnCount`. This starts a new night without resetting the battle's cumulative player-turn count.
2. Start the **player** turn. Increment controller-turn identity, refill player AP to **10**, and reset only living field units of the entering side. Run only explicitly enabled start-boundary effects. Publish before accepting commands. Beginning an enemy phase performs its eligibility reset without creating/refilling a budget.
3. Player chooses A/B/etc. Each accepted action spends shared AP and consumes only that actor's opportunity. Its result completes and plays back; control remains in the same player turn, even if that actor dies. Units need not be used in any order. AP 0 or no legal action does not auto-end the player turn.
4. Accept manual `EndPlayerTurn` at most once for this turn identity, including an early end with unused AP/actions. Resolve enabled owner-end effects, increment completed player turns once and settle outcome. If combat survives, start the enemy phase, **including after completed-in-night reaches 8**. Do not switch to day yet.
5. Enemy phase chooses one eligible unit action per automatic step: attack a legal current target, otherwise move if possible, otherwise no action. It publishes actual actions and waits through existing result presentation. New same-phase summons remain deferred. When no available enemy can act, complete that phase once; no player-turn counter increment occurs here.
6. If completed-in-night is below 8, begin the next player turn. If it is 8, the enemy-phase completion instead runs night-end cleanup and changes to day/deployment. Expire the night-scoped effects, clear round item state, recall surviving non-main units, advance PreparationIndex once, and publish NightEnded through the existing projection/transaction path. No ninth player turn starts. **[Proposed]** Keep this cleanup in the accepted enemy-phase completion transition so repeated advancement cannot repeat the same day change.
7. On the next accepted EnterCombat from daytime deployment, record the new night baseline and start player turn 1 of that night. For example, cumulative counts 8 and 16 finish different nights; the night-local displayed count resets while the cumulative player count remains monotonic.

Per-unit action completion, player-turn completion, enemy-phase completion, day transition and battle completion are distinct result/event meanings. A “round” is not introduced as a synonym for a turn. `PreparationIndex` remains the deployment/night-cycle identity used by existing item state; it is not a player-turn counter. Derive `NightEndsAfterEnemyPhase` from the active enemy boundary and completed-in-night == 8 rather than storing a second countdown/boolean authority.

If the battle ends during player turn 8, its enemy response, or night-end effect resolution, completion wins: do not synthesize later phases, day, recall or another AP refill. If no enemy action is possible after player turn 8, its accepted phase-completion step still performs the day boundary; it must not wait for an enemy action that cannot occur.

### 4.4 End-boundary order and termination

**[Existing]** `EndTurn` currently seals movement and settles own-turn status, refills AP, updates counters/night and thorn lifetime, records TurnEnded, heals Regeneration holders, resolves tornado, then pending weak ground. Death can bypass the unit-end boundary. These differences matter when assigning new timings.

**[Proposed] Player end:** validate intent/turn identity, resolve enabled owner-end work, increment player-completed count once, settle deaths/rewards/outcome and publish end facts. If still in combat, start the enemy response even when the night-local count reaches 8. Unit action completion never calls this sequence; actor death alone never synthesizes it. A terminal action ends combat without a player-end tick/refill.

**[Proposed] Enemy end and day:** finish enabled enemy-owner-end work and any approved post-response world effects, resolving their causal deaths/outcome. If combat survives and the night-local count is 8, expire supplies, clear night-scoped item state, clear other enabled night-scoped state, recall survivors, increment PreparationIndex once, set deployment/day and publish NightEnded. Otherwise prepare the next player start. Preserve the existing protection against emitting deployment before cleanup completes. No extra player-count increment or player AP refill occurs during this sequence. Disabled unit-timed hooks are never called. If an end effect ends battle, completion supersedes day/start and rewards settle only once.

**[Proposed] Reactivation ordering contract:** each feature specifies whether it runs before or after increment, its observed controller, recipient set, newborn-state treatment and position relative to other enabled effects. Local causal chains finish before terminal evaluation where existing contracts require a batch (weak-ground simultaneous collapses), but no subsequent unrelated periodic feature starts after terminal outcome. Death/drop/kill attribution is settled at existing causal boundaries; final clear rewards/experience are packaged once. Preserve the current damage/arrival/death pipeline, not a second end-turn reward system. Railway/tornado order is an explicit reactivation decision; nothing authorizes blindly retaining or swapping their old order.

### 4.5 AI adaptation

**[Existing]** `EnemyAiPolicy` reuses attack preview/geometry, avoids allied collateral, scores actual damage and badge breaks, chooses hostile units before headquarters, and has deterministic aim ties. `EnemyMovementPathfinder` looks for attack positions and walks an affordable prefix. `EnemyAiTurnService` currently attacks after movement if possible, then ends that unit turn.

**[Confirmed]** Enemy behavior is initially unbudgeted and simple: a legal current attack target means attack; otherwise attempt movement, never move then attack in the same phase. **[Proposed]** Iterate eligible living field enemies in stable EntityId order; use current target geometry/hostility/capability to select a legal aim, and reuse pursuit/path legality when none exists. Existing target ranking may choose among legal targets, but it must not redefine “has a target” as “will deal positive damage”: a legal zero-damage attack still counts as an attack opportunity. Do not require `CanAffordAttack` from the old per-unit AP model. Avoid a new global tactical optimizer.

The enemy route uses the same normalized L-path geometry/category allowance, with no AP debit. Re-evaluate world state before each next unit. A unit that moved becomes unavailable for attack until the next enemy start. New units created during this phase are deferred. Stable iteration is deterministic execution order, not a stored unit initiative schedule and not authority over player choices.

A candidate with no legal action is skipped during that decision pass; if no enemy action is possible, publish an accepted phase-completion transition. Do not consume a unit's action for a rejected request or spin forever on the same failed action. The existing `CombatAdvanceJob` demands accepted progress, so automatic inability must produce a real skip/phase-completion decision or select another valid candidate, not publish endless rejected Advances. No private AI cursor should become a second state authority. Reactions and enabled special abilities use their own explicit admission rules; disabled necromancy must not fall through to ordinary boss attacks contrary to `UnitBehavior`.

## 6. Migration sequence and maintainability

### 6.1 High-level sequence and removal criteria

This is dependency ordering, not a detailed implementation plan or authorization to begin a phase.

1. **Use the confirmed operating profile.** Player AP 10, unbudgeted simple enemies, manual player end, owner-start eligibility reset, day after the eighth enemy response, and effectful L-shaped diagonals are fixed. Record the proposed deferral/lifetime subset and acceptance examples; do not reopen those settled values. Special boss and priority/periodic redesigns remain later work.
2. **Change authoritative contracts together.** Add shared budget, controller-turn identity and unit action-use facts; update expected/final validation, preparation and all copying/publication constructors. Retire unit AP at the same integration boundary, not by synchronizing both models. Add state checks to existing Buffer preparation, not game-rule calculation to Buffer.
3. **Migrate actions and automatic controller lifecycle.** Reuse movement/attack/effect execution and existing Jobs. Implement standalone fixed-price weighted movement, single-use eligibility, player turn end and approved enemy behavior. Disconnect deferred feature producers as well as their timers. Remove attack-confirmed movement and checkpoint return.
4. **Hand off consumer migration and adapt owned data.** This worker handles authorized Core/Buffer/data changes and records old-to-new contracts for the View owner. That owner implements View/input/tutorial adaptation. Core source can be delivered first in isolation, with compilation/test limits reported; integrated HUD/selection/playback acceptance requires the external delivery. No compatibility branch may retain unit initiative as the real action authority.
5. **Retire obsolete runtime paths.** Remove timeline query/registration/schedule/participant facts and unit resource fields only after callers, validators, snapshots, result handling and relevant tests are migrated. Search production, tests and serialized references. Preserve necessary battle seeds, positions, animation sequences, Framework ordering and content identities. Retained source must compile against current contracts; remove obsolete system adapters without retaining a hidden scheduler or redesigning content to keep it operational. No speculative extraction is required for disabled content.
6. **Verify the agreed scope and stop.** Run focused contract/calculator/Buffer/composition/consumer checks and required integration acceptance for the actual implementation changes. Content deletion, replacement or reactivation is separate planner-owned work and does not gate completion of this migration.

Removal gates: no production unit-budget reads/writes; no `ActiveActorId`-based player admission; no unit-schedule authority/forecast consumer; no checkpoint return producers or consumers; no accepted action can charge without matching eligibility/world publication; no deferred effect arms/consumes silently; no stale unit-turn tutorial or presentation callback; unchanged exit/reward semantics remain protected.
